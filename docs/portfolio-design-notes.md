## Content decisions

- Events lead selected work and have a direct navigation link. Software and music each have their own substantive section, rather than acting as footnotes.
- Spotify: 400 attendees, $25k budget, 20 on-site staff, 10 vendors.
- Gig App launch: 120 attendees in a 120-capacity rented Williamsburg bar, ticketed, with all attendance through organic outreach.
- Ambrook: newly established monthly jazz night, 140 attendees at the first event. No fabricated dates, future bookings, or growth claims.
- Chobani: a planned series of $250k events, approved to source vendors and finalize budgets, that did not proceed to production. Do not count those budgets as spend or list the events as delivered. The $250k wording refers to the scale of the planned events, not an invented total series budget.
- The Ambrook and Williamsburg features use the event photos Benny supplied. Chobani is framed as concepts and planning without repeated cancellation labels.
- Gallery, Gig App, Minecraft Plugins, Jetside, tour assistants, and existing project routes remain discoverable. Previously removed projects remain removed.
- Current employer is Ambrook. The site presents Benny as an engineer, event producer, and musician without making the whole site specific to one job opening.
