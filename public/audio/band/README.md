# Band instrument samples

Source: [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments),
N. Brosowsky and contributors. Licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Original brass, piano, and contrabass: VSCO 2 / Versilian Studios.
Saxophone: Karoryfer. Source files are the corresponding instrument and pitch
in the repository's `samples` directory (`As` means B-flat).

Modified for this site: trimmed to 2.2 seconds, peak normalized to −3 dB,
resampled to mono 32 kHz, encoded as 64 kbps MP3, and given a short end fade.
The solo trombone adds a crossfade to its loop at playback time.

The arrangement is in `lib/band/score.js`; the walking bassline was written
by Benny Conn. No recordings of Benny's playing are used in this interaction.
